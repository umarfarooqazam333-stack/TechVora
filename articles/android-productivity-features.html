10 Practical Android Productivity Features Worth Enabling

Android • By John Smith • Jun 18, 2026 • 6 min read

This article highlights ten settings and features available on modern Android devices that can help you be more efficient day-to-day.

Quick settings and gestures

Customize quick toggles for fast access to commonly used features. System gestures such as double-tap to wake save time and reduce strain.

Notification management

Prioritize notifications for important apps and silence noisy apps. Use notification channels to fine-tune alerts.

Do not disturb schedules

Set up DND schedules for focus time or sleeping hours. Allow priority contacts to break through when necessary.
